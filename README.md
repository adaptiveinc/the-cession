# The Cession

An explorable IFRS 17 learning game about loss-recovery components on
reinsurance held.

Two Zurich houses, two ways a loss is born. At Limmat Assurance a cohort is
onerous the day it signs, and you play the cession up and down at the deal
desk to see what the cover buys and what it costs. Across town at Mutual &
Torres a claims shock lands on a book that was priced right, and the loss has
to be mirrored contract by contract. Ten meetings turn both losses into booked
recovery income, and show where the cost of cover lives.

**Play it here: https://adaptiveinc.github.io/the-cession/**

The game is a single self-contained HTML file, built from modular source
chunks. No install, no backend, works on phones.
